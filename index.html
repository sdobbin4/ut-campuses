One University, Five Campuses

The University of Tennessee System stretches about 350 miles, from the Smoky Mountain foothills to the Mississippi River. Each of its five campuses began as something else: a frontier college, a Methodist women's school, a Baptist institute. This project maps where they are, when they joined UT, and how many students they serve today.

The system at a glance

UT is a statewide system. Five campuses each have their own chancellor, all reporting to the UT System president. Only Knoxville was part of UT from the start. Each of the other four joined later, by merger, by state takeover, or by acquisition, between 1911 and 2021.

Who studies where

Knoxville, the flagship, enrolls about 62% of all UT students. Chattanooga set a campus record in Fall 2025, passing 12,000 students for the first time.

Total headcount, Fall 2025, as reported by the UT System. The five campuses add up exactly to the 64,866 systemwide total.
